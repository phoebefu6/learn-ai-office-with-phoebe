Learn AI Office with Phoebe

Six sessions, 45 minutes each, and one real weekly report you build across all of them. This is the honest version of the course. You have been told this technology makes you forty percent faster, and on one task in a lab that is true. Across a measured working week it comes out nearer two percent. Nobody lied to you, and understanding exactly why is worth more than any prompt library.

The six sessions 📄 · one weekly report, built across all of them

Pick the report you actually produce every week and quietly resent. Not a sample. Every session moves that one closer to running itself, and by session 6 it is saved, repeatable and honest about what you will never automate.

⏳ Session 1 · easy

The hour back

Where the time actually goes, why only 340 minutes of your 2,400-minute week is reachable at all, and the four-part ask that earns most of what is left. Plus the meeting claim that is measurably false.

▶ Start here 📝 Session 2 · easy

Documents

Drafting, rewriting and summarizing without losing the exception that matters. The real failure mode on long documents is omission, not invention, and a thing that is missing never looks wrong.

the missing bit 🔢 Session 3 · medium

Spreadsheets

Formulas you can defend. What Microsoft's own COPILOT function documentation forbids, why it tells you not to use it for numerical calculations, and the gap between 57.2 percent and a human expert's 71.3.

show the working 📬 Session 4 · medium

Inbox and meetings

Email that does not cost you trust, and there is a study on that. Meeting notes and what they quietly drop. Plus the finding nobody repeats: meetings did not go down.

trust 🧾 Session 5 · hands-on

The report

Assemble the whole thing end to end, with every figure traceable to a cell you can open. This is the session where the artifact becomes real and the traceability discipline gets its teeth.

the real build 🔁 Session 6 · hands-on

Make it repeatable

Saved prompts, habit over heroics, and what you should never automate. Oversharing, prompt injection from ordinary office files, and the honest list of things to keep your hands on.
